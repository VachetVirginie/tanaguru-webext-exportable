- visible notice that the extension is an unofficial local derivative;
- global collection of Tanaguru analysis results;
- generated readable RGAA report;
- RGAA criterion mapping, criterion descriptions, and links to the official RGAA documentation;

- removal of visible JSON, CSV, and PDF export buttons.
